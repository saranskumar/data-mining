1. WHAT THE PROJECT DOES (30-second version)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Two parts, one web app:

  A. FORENSIC DETECTION - tells whether an image looks AI-generated.
     Classical data-mining pipeline: hand-made features -> scaling -> PCA ->
     classifiers (Decision Tree, MLP, baselines) -> K-Means clustering.

  B. INVISIBLE WATERMARKING - hides a 16-bit message inside a photo and reads
     it back later. A small neural network (encoder + decoder, PyTorch).
     The photo keeps its original size and the change cannot be seen.

The verdict shown in the app comes from the MLP ("MLP main"). The other three
models are shown next to it for comparison.


2. DATA
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Main dataset : CIFAKE - 120,000 images, 32x32.
               100,000 train / 20,000 test. REAL = CIFAR-10 photos,
               FAKE = generated by Stable Diffusion 1.4.
               Source used: Hugging Face mirror dragonintelligence/CIFAKE-image-dataset
               (its labels are flipped vs. ours; the code converts them:
               0 = real, 1 = fake).
New-generator test set (modern, "out-of-distribution"):
               ~5,000 images from GPT Image 2, Nano Banana 2, FLUX.2 and
               similar, plus real photos. Resized to 32x32.
               Why: CIFAKE is old; today's generators look different. This set
               checks whether the detector still works on them.
Where        : dataset/ (about 6 GB, NOT stored in git).
               dataset/download_datasets.py downloads everything.
               dataset/DATASETS.md lists sources, licences, label rules.


3. THE PIPELINE, STEP BY STEP
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Everything lives in code/. Each step is a numbered script. The Train tab in the
web app can run every one of them (or run_all.py runs 01 to 08 together).

 01_preprocess.py          Load CIFAKE, resize to 32x32, split train / val /
                           test (10% of train kept for validation), save as
                           uint8 arrays (saves memory).
 02_feature_extraction.py  Turn each image into 176 numbers:
                             - noise residue (image minus its blurred copy)
                             - frequency features (FFT radial bins, DCT)
                             - colour histograms and moments (RGB, HSV)
                             - GLCM texture statistics
                           AI images leave different traces in these.
 03_pca_reduce.py          StandardScaler (zero mean, unit variance), then PCA
                           keeping 95% of the variance: 176 -> 73 components.
 04_classify.py            Trains and compares:
                             Dummy (baseline), Logistic Regression,
                             Decision Tree (Gini), Decision Tree (entropy),
                             MLP (128, 64).
                           Also: 5-fold cross-validation, ROC curves,
                           confusion matrices, McNemar test, ablation
                           (which feature group matters), error analysis.
 05_cluster.py             K-Means for k = 2..12 on the PCA features; elbow,
                           silhouette, purity, ARI, NMI.
 06_watermark_train.py     Trains the watermark encoder + decoder (GPU).
 07_watermark_eval.py      Watermark benchmark at 32x32 under attacks.
 07b_watermark_fullres_eval.py
                           Watermark test on real full-size photos.
 08_hybrid_eval.py         How watermarking affects the forensic verdicts.
 data_ood/build_modern.py  Builds the modern-generator arrays.
 09_ood_eval.py            Runs the detectors on the modern-generator images.
 app/make_samples.py       Builds the sample gallery for the demo.
 run_all.py                Runs 01 to 08 in order.

Supporting code: code/utils/ (features, metrics, plots, watermark model,
full-size watermark apply, attacks), code/configs/config.yaml (all settings),
code/tests/ (17 automated tests, run with: pytest tests/ -v).


4. HOW THE WATERMARK WORKS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Embedding:
  1. Shrink the photo to 32x32.
  2. The encoder network takes that image + the 16 message bits and produces
     a tiny change pattern (never more than +/-0.03 per pixel, average zero,
     so there is no colour tint).
  3. That pattern is enlarged smoothly to the photo's real size and added.
     The output stays the original size.
  4. "Perceptual masking": less change in flat areas (sky, paper) where it
     could be noticed, more in textured areas.
  5. Embed-and-verify: the app decodes its own result, also after a JPEG-80
     save, and raises the strength only if needed.
Decoding: shrink to 32x32 -> decoder network -> 16 bits -> compare with the
messages embedded in this demo (stored in outputs/wm_registry.json) and name
the match. A match needs at least 15 of 16 bits (false-positive chance about
1 in 3,800 per image, exact binomial threshold 0.9375).
Training trick list (why it works): a noise layer during training (noise,
blur, resize, crop, JPEG-like), quantisation with straight-through gradient,
a learned spatial embedding of the message, reflect padding, zero-mean change,
bit accuracy reached 1.000 on validation.


5. RESULTS TO QUOTE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. Forensic detection on CIFAKE test set (20,000 images)
     Model                 Accuracy   ROC-AUC
     MLP (128, 64)           93.45%     0.983    <- main model, F1 0.933
     Logistic regression     89.82%     0.963
     Decision tree (Gini)    85.07%     0.919
     Decision tree (entropy) 84.70%     0.920
     Dummy baseline          50.96%     0.500
   MLP cross-validation: 93.35% +/- 0.11%. McNemar test: MLP vs trees p < 0.001.
   Most important feature: fft_radial_bin7 (one frequency band, importance 0.42).

B. K-Means clustering (unsupervised)
   Best k = 2: silhouette 0.106, purity 0.645, ARI 0.084, NMI 0.062.
   Meaning: real and fake do NOT separate cleanly without labels.

C. Modern generators (the honest limit)
   All models drop to about 50% balanced accuracy (= guessing).
   MLP: 93.45% on CIFAKE -> 49.7% on new generators; it catches only ~24% of
   modern fakes. Reason: the detector learned Stable Diffusion 1.4 traces.
   (Per-generator numbers: Results tab, "per generator" table.)

D. Watermark, 32x32 benchmark
   Clean bit accuracy 99.99%. Survives resize, crop and blur at 32x32.
   JPEG at 32x32 destroys it (a 32 px image has nothing left to hide in).
   False positives on unmarked images: 0.

E. Watermark on 150 real full-size photos (verified mode, strength 0.75)
   Image quality  : PSNR 43.2 dB, SSIM 0.998 (not visible to the eye)
   Detected clean : 100%
   After JPEG 70  : 99.3%    After JPEG 50: 99.3%
   After resize to half: 100%
   After 80% centre crop: 0%  (cropping breaks it - known limitation)
   False positives on unmarked photos: 0 of 150.


6. THE WEB APP (what to open and show)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Start (from the repo, PowerShell):
    cd code
    python -m streamlit run app\demo.py
Open http://localhost:8501 (if that port is busy: add --server.port 8502).

Tabs and a good demo order:
  1. Detect     Pick a sample (real vs AI) or upload an image. Shows the
                verdict card (MLP), a bar for all four models, and an
                expander "Why?" with feature-group evidence. Batch mode scans
                many files and exports a CSV.
  2. Watermark  Choose a photo, type a message (default HybridMarker), set
                strength. Shows size kept, PSNR, original / watermarked /
                difference x10, a PNG download, and a robustness check
                (pick an attack, see if the mark survives).
  3. Decode     Upload that PNG (or a JPEG re-save of it). It names the
                hidden message and shows matching bits. Try the gallery item
                "HybridMarker (re-saved JPEG 75)" to show it offline, and any
                normal photo to show "No watermark found".
  4. Train      Run the whole pipeline or one stage from the browser. Each
                stage card has options, a built / not built chip and a Run
                button. A live log, elapsed time and Stop button appear above.
                One job at a time; it keeps running if you refresh.
  5. Results    KPI tiles (accuracy, AUC, new-generator score, watermark
                survival), all result tables and the figures.
  6. Logs       Step-by-step log with timings of every run in the session.
                Every tab also has its own "Process log".
Sidebar shows whether the models are loaded and whether a job is running.

8. HOW TO RUN FROM ZERO (short)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

    .\venv\Scripts\Activate.ps1
    pip install -r requirements.txt
    pip install torch torchvision --index-url https://download.pytorch.org/whl/cu121
    python dataset\download_datasets.py

    python data_ood\build_modern.py --per-source 1000

    python run_all.py --subset 5000 --workers 4 --skip-watermark
Our machine: Windows 11, RTX 2050 (4 GB), 16 GB RAM, Python 3.12.
The full run took many hours (decision-tree grid search is the slow part;
use --resume on 04_classify.py to reuse the saved trees).


9. LIMITATIONS (say these out loud - they show understanding)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - The detector only works well on the generator it was trained on
    (Stable Diffusion 1.4). On GPT Image 2 / Nano Banana 2 / FLUX it is near
    chance. A "real" verdict is never proof of authenticity.
  - All analysis happens on 32x32 images, so fine detail is lost.
  - The watermark does not survive cropping and is destroyed by JPEG at 32 px.
    Full-size photos survive JPEG 50-90 and half-size resizing.
  - Decoding names a message only if it was embedded with this demo (registry);
    otherwise it reports found / not found against a typed key.
  - The watermark is a prototype, not a secure provenance standard: someone
    who knows the method could try to remove it.


10. QUESTIONS YOU MAY BE ASKED
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q: Why PCA?          176 features are correlated; PCA removes redundancy and
                     speeds up the classifiers (95% variance in 73 components).
Q: Why a Decision Tree if the MLP is better?   Trees are explainable (feature
                     importance, the fft band), the MLP is more accurate.
Q: What is McNemar?  A test of whether two classifiers make different errors
                     on the same test images (p < 0.001 = really different).
Q: Why does K-Means fail to separate?   Unsupervised clusters follow image
                     content (animals, vehicles), not real vs fake; purity is
                     only 0.645.
Q: Why does it fail on new generators?   Distribution shift: it learned old
                     generator artefacts; new models do not have them.
Q: Why is the watermark invisible?   Change <= 0.03 per pixel, zero-mean,
                     smooth, reduced in flat areas: PSNR 43 dB.
Q: How is a false alarm avoided?   Need 15 of 16 bits; measured 0 false
                     positives on 150 unmarked photos.
